What to listen for, in order of how likely it is to be wrong.

  1. The ambience seam. Starts 4 s before the loop point. A click at the wrap is the one defect the numbers say is plausible.
  2. Jump. Its first sample is 0.084 rather than near silence, so it may click on trigger. Every other cue starts under 0.007.
  3. The worst case. Not whether it clips — that is measured — but whether six cues at once reads as one event or as noise.
  4. Every cue against its event name. A measurement cannot hear a jump sound on a landing.

# Phase 10 — criterion 10.13: the cross-phase review sweep

> Every recorded-but-not-fixed Codex finding from phases 1–9, re-read and dispositioned.
> Sibling of [phase-10-ship.md](phase-10-ship.md); split out to keep both under the 400-line rule.

## Method, and why it is not a glob

**All 35 files in `docs/reviews/` were enumerated by name**, not matched by `*-plan.md`. That glob
misses Phase 5's three split records — `phase-05-plan-r1-r2.md`, `-r3-r5.md`, `-r6-r8.md` — and
`r1-r2.md:222-226` carries the single finding that matters most. It also misses the seven
`session-*` records, which are reviews of bug-fix sessions rather than phases and which contain two
of the sharpest findings in the corpus.

The corpus is **5,179 lines**. Every line carrying a non-applied disposition marker (`rejected`,
`recorded`, `not fixed`, `not applied`, `deferred`, `declined`, `partially applied`, `no change`,
`deliberate`, `carried`, `out of scope`) was extracted — **99 lines across 34 files**, Phase 10's own
plan review excluded — and each was read in place with its surrounding table row.

Each is dispositioned as one of:

- **STILL TRUE AND SHIPPING** — the finding holds today and the public repository carries it.
- **FIXED SINCE** — closed by later work, with the file that closes it named and checked.
- **NO LONGER APPLIES** — the premise changed.
- **DELIBERATE** — a recorded decision, re-affirmed here rather than re-litigated.

⚠️ **Every "fixed since" was verified against the current tree**, not against a later document's
claim that it was fixed. Two of them turned out to be claims rather than fixes when this project
last checked (`session-bugfix-tiers-impl.md` §Y7 — *"a disposition recorded APPLIED that was not
applied is worse than one recorded unfixed"*), which is exactly why.

## 🔴 The headline: the art pipeline the repository documents does not exist

**`docs/reviews/phase-04-impl.md:26` (finding 5, MAJOR) and
`docs/reviews/phase-05-plan-r1-r2.md:222-226`.** Recorded twice, in two phases, by two reviews.

> *"The documented clean-clone pipeline does not exist. `ASSET-PIPELINE.md` promises `assets:fetch`
> and `assets:verify`; `package.json` defines neither."*

**Disposition: STILL TRUE AND SHIPPING.** Verified 2026-08-26 —
`Object.keys(package.json.scripts).filter(/fetch|verify/)` returns **nothing**. `.gitignore:12`
still tells a reader that `_generated/` is *"regenerated by `npm run assets:fetch` from the request
ids in docs/GENERATION-LOG.md"*, and that script has never existed.

Phase 5's review was explicit that this was not optional scope: *"`assets:fetch`/`assets:verify` …
are binding Phase 4 debt, not optional scope."* It was not done in Phase 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 or 10.

**What it actually costs, stated plainly:** the public repository **cannot reconstruct its own art
from its recorded provenance.** The `request_id` of every generation is preserved, and the raws those
ids point at live only in gitignored `_generated/`; the generators are not seed-deterministic, so a
prompt cannot stand in for a raw. A fresh clone can rebuild the *bundle* byte-for-byte (that is
criterion 10.9, and it passes 62/62) and cannot rebuild a single *sprite sheet*.

This is why **criterion 10.9 was amended by owner ruling** rather than satisfied, and Codex's
objection to that amendment is recorded verbatim in the gate table: *"weakened, not discharged."* It
is also why [ASSETS-LICENSE.md](../../ASSETS-LICENSE.md) says so in the licence itself rather than
leaving a reader to discover it — the honest answer to *"can I make my own version of these?"* is,
from this repository alone, no.

**Not fixed in Phase 10, deliberately.** Writing `assets:fetch` is a Phase 4 pipeline feature and a
fal-spend question, not a shipping task, and inventing it in the final phase would be exactly the
scope growth this project's stop conditions exist to prevent. It is the top item of carried debt and
is recorded as such in HANDOFF.

## What this sweep did not do

It read **dispositions**, not every finding. A finding recorded **APPLIED** was re-checked only where
a later document, a criterion, or this phase's own work touched it. `session-bugfix-tiers-impl.md`'s
Y7 is the standing proof that an APPLIED disposition can be false, so this is a real limit and it is
stated rather than left for someone to assume otherwise *(vault 9.3)*. Closing it properly would mean
re-verifying all ~200 applied findings against the tree, which is a session of its own.

**Count:** 99 non-applied disposition lines across 34 files → **6 still true and shipping** (one of
them owner-facing rather than a defect), **12 fixed since** (each verified against the tree), **13
deliberate and re-affirmed**, **1 no longer applies**. The rest are tally lines and section headings
rather than findings.
